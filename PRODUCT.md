# Product

## Register

product

## Users

NE clients who manage their own website content, and Neu Entity administrators or operators who oversee multiple client websites. They need to understand a site's current state quickly, make routine content and configuration changes safely, and move between clients without losing context.

## Product Purpose

NE Website Manager is the operating workspace for Neu Entity-managed websites. It should replace fragmented manual updates with clear, guided workflows for publishing content, arranging site structure, handling enquiries, reviewing performance, and maintaining integrations. Success means routine work takes fewer steps, users can see the effect of a change before publishing, and operators can manage many client sites without treating every task as a raw database record.

## Brand Personality

Capable, calm, empowering. The product should feel like a dependable professional tool that makes complex website operations understandable without becoming sterile, intimidating, or over-designed.

## Anti-references

- A generic CRUD admin made of repetitive tables, forms, and identical white cards.
- A dense enterprise control panel that exposes implementation details instead of user goals.
- Decorative dashboard concepts whose charts and controls do not perform real work.
- A completely free-form page builder that can silently break Neu Entity's custom-coded client sites.
- Hidden automation, ambiguous icon-only actions, and controls that appear interactive but do nothing.

## Design Principles

1. Organize around jobs, not database entities: publishing, arranging, reviewing, responding, and improving.
2. Prefer direct manipulation with safe recovery: drag to arrange, preview the result, support undo, and retain an accessible keyboard alternative.
3. Keep site context visible: the current client, affected website surface, publish state, and next consequence should never be ambiguous.
4. Show before committing: pair editing with live or representative previews and make publish effects explicit.
5. Automate repetitive setup while preserving operator control through templates, defaults, bulk actions, and guided workflows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Formal WCAG certification is not a release requirement, but the dashboard must remain operable and legible for its working users. Maintain readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, labelled controls, reduced-motion support, non-colour-only status communication, and keyboard-accessible alternatives for drag-and-drop workflows.
